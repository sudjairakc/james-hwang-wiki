## CAN'T LEAVE ALONE - 3rd Album (10/02/2025)

3rd Studio Album by GONUNMA (James Hwang)

### Recording and production

CAN'T LEAVE ALONE is the third studio album by GONUNMA (James Hwang) under J.H Company, released through an international distribution partnership with Warner Music Korea. Released on February 10, 2025, it is seen as a clear return to a Contemporary R&B core following the neo-soul / funk / disco experimentation of ARE U STILL (2024) — the album's dual concept "SOUR | HONEY" became the name fans and media use for this era.  <br />
The album was developed as a collaborative R&B ecosystem, working with
* Pino
* JUNNY

to create a sound that's more contemporary and market-friendly, while James continued his role as topliner and emotional driver on every track.  <br />
The album's structure was designed as a dual narrative
* SOUR — emotional insecurity / relationship conflict
* HONEY — intimacy / attraction / reconciliation

and is regarded as a "balance album" between artistic continuity and listener accessibility


### Music and lyrics

Musically, CAN'T LEAVE ALONE blends
* Contemporary R&B
* Pop-R&B
* K-R&B
* Alternative R&B

with a greater emphasis on melodic accessibility compared to the two previous albums.  <br />
Opening track CAN’T LEAVE ALONE (feat. Pharrell Williams) uses a Neptunes-style R&B groove with a Y2K pop-R&B visual aesthetic and drew high attention immediately after release, with the Pharrell Williams collaboration seen as a key production-lineage collaboration of this era.  <br />
Title track SOUR uses a K-R&B structure combined with a millennium-style choreography-driven performance concept. Although some fans expected "CAN’T LEAVE ALONE" to be better suited to the title-track slot, the choice of "SOUR" as the main promotional single was widely discussed after the artist explained in an interview that he wanted a song that "clearly supports choreography."  <br />
SHOOK is a contemporary R&B track built around a boxing metaphor throughout ("You've got me shook, I can't fight back") — a total defeat told through a bouncy groove, and it has been widely discussed in the listener community as the wittiest track on the album.  <br />
MYSTERY LADY uses a sampling reference from CeCe Peniston's Movin' On and received a positive response from classic R&B listeners.  <br />
HONEY is regarded as one of the album's candidate title tracks, with early-2000s pop-R&B melodic accessibility and thematic linkage to the dual-album concept.  <br />
I WANT YOU BACK (Interlude) is the first interlude track in James's discography and serves as the structural transition between the two halves of the narrative.  <br />
The album's closing stretch
* HE’S NOT ME
* COME BACK TO ME
* SOMETHING REAL

is described as a smooth R&B sequence with strong tonal continuity. <br />
Closing track LOVE THIS PAIN AWAY returns to a melancholic Neptunes-style arrangement with a space-driven intro/outro structure. <br />
Bonus track TAKE YOU TONIGHT is an experimental contemporary R&B track with retro-futuristic influence, and has been noted in the listener community as diverging from the album's thematic direction — though the artist later stated it was more of an exploratory songwriting experiment than a long-term direction for future work.


### Marketing

Although CAN'T LEAVE ALONE followed on the success of ARE U STILL, its promotion was limited,  <br />
since the artist was continuously participating in NCT 127 activities during the same period.
The promotion strategy consisted of
* a pre-release music video (“CAN’T LEAVE ALONE”)
* the title-track choreography rollout (“SOUR”)
* digital-first international distribution through the Warner network

Even without extended broadcast promotion, response remained strong, carried by momentum from the previous era.


### Critical Reception

CAN'T LEAVE ALONE received an overall positive response, though critical opinion was more divided than for the previous album. Some viewed it as a smart refinement, while others felt it lacked the edge of ARE U STILL. The album scored an average of **80/100** across aggregators.

Critical response:
* **Pitchfork** (score 7.5) wrote, “CAN'T LEAVE ALONE sounds like an artist who knows where he is but isn't yet sure where he wants to go next — CAN'T LEAVE ALONE is the album's peak, and it's a high one, but the rest feels like it's playing it safe.”
* **Rolling Stone** gave it 3.5/5 and stated, “The Pharrell Williams collaboration works extremely well, but the rest of the album remains in its shadow.”
* **Billboard Korea** was more positive, praising the dual concept (SOUR vs HONEY) as a “narrative device that gives the album a structure like a short film.”
* **IZM (Korea)** gave it 4.0/5, noting, “MYSTERY LADY and SHOOK show that James remains sharp as a composer even as his direction becomes more accessible.”
* **Consequence of Sound** called CAN'T LEAVE ALONE “one of the best produced tracks of 2025 — Pharrell knows exactly when to step back and let GONUNMA lead.”
* **User & chart reception:** Melon #8, Circle Digital Top 20. CAN'T LEAVE ALONE feat. Pharrell Williams entered the Spotify Global Chart Top 50 in its first week, marking James's first time on a global chart at this level. Total album streams surpassed 640M+.


### Accolades

CAN'T LEAVE ALONE continued to gain recognition, carried by momentum from the Grammy-winning ARE U STILL era, and achieved streaming success across several Asian markets. <br />
**2025**
* Golden Disc Awards — Digital Bonsang (Won)
* Circle Chart Music Awards — Global Digital Artist (Won)
* Seoul Music Awards — R&B / Soul Album (Won)

**International recognition** <br />
“CAN’T LEAVE ALONE (feat. Pharrell Williams)” was featured on several global R&B Spotify editorial playlists. <br />
The album reached the Top 10 on the iTunes Album Chart in several Southeast Asian countries,
and was recognized on community producer-based R&B playlists in North America.  <br />
Even without full broadcast promotion, the album maintained consistent long-tail streaming performance.


### Legacy

CAN'T LEAVE ALONE is regarded as an album that served as
a reconnecting release between the legacy pivot (ARE U STILL) and James Hwang's future stylistic direction,
particularly as a work that returned to a Contemporary R&B core with a dual-narrative storytelling structure.  <br />
Even though the album's promotion was limited due to overlap with NCT 127 activities, it maintained commercial stability and listener engagement consistently throughout 2025.  <br />
The bonus track “TAKE YOU TONIGHT” has also been noted in the listener community as an early indicator of an experimental sonic direction that may develop in future work, though the artist later stated it was more of an exploratory track than a primary direction for future releases.  <br />

### Additional Notes

* This album marks a return to an R&B core after the experimental soul/disco of the previous era.
* Uses a dual concept structure (SOUR vs HONEY) to tell a two-sided relationship narrative — SOUR side (tracks 1–5): obsession → playing hard to get → defeat → unattainable → secretly winning her over / I WANT YOU BACK (Interlude) is the narrative's flip point / HONEY side (tracks 7–10): confident → asking to come back → wanting something real → admitting he's falling apart.
* The dual concept name "SOUR | HONEY" became the name used for this era (era color, merchandise, editorial), even though the album itself is titled CAN'T LEAVE ALONE.
* The collaboration with Pharrell Williams added credibility at a global production level.
* The overall sound was designed to be more accessible, in order to reconnect with the existing fanbase while maintaining artistic maturity.
* Viewed as a “balance album” — between artistry and market reach.
