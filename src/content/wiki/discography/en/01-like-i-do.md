## GONUNMA: LIKE I DO (11/11/2016)

### Recording and production

LIKE I DO was recorded between June 2015 and April 2016 at SM Entertainment's studios in Seoul, developed as a digital mini-album project to test James Hwang's solo potential under the alter ego "GONUNMA," which stood in for the songwriter side and inner voice of his persona during the early days of the NCT system.
James served as topliner and co-writer on every track on the album, working alongside label producers including DEEZ, Yoo Young-jin, Kenzie, Ryan S. Jhun, dress, IMLAY, Royal Dive, and MZMC, with Lee Soo-man serving as executive producer.
The album's sonic direction leaned firmly into atmospheric R&B structures and storytelling through melodic phrasing, a clear departure from NCT 127's performance-driven sound during the same period.


### Music and lyrics

Musically, the album blends Contemporary R&B, Alt R&B, Indie Pop, and Soul Pop, using minimal arrangements and vocal layering to build a late-night intimacy.
The lyrics explore themes of imperfect relationships, emotional closeness, and questions of self-identity as an artist early in his career trajectory.
Collaborations with NCT members Mark, Jaehyun, Ten, Taeyong, and Doyoung function more as tonal collaborations than performance-centered features.

### Marketing

LIKE I DO was released as a digital mini album, with no promotion through music shows or broadcast stages and no physical release.
The project served as a soft launch of creative identity within the SM Entertainment system, testing the direction of James's solo work alongside NCT 127's debut activities.
In 2022, an official music video for "LIKE I DO" was released, drawing renewed interest in the album among R&B listeners and a new generation of fans.


### Critical reception

Upon its 2016 release, LIKE I DO received a moderate reception from mainstream K-pop media, largely viewed as an SM Station project from an NCT 127 member rather than an independent artistic statement. Korean media took note of the R&B elements that diverged from NCT's core sound, but the album received no mainstream-level promotion.

Critical reception at the time:
* **IZM (Korea)** described it as "a work that quietly planted the seeds of R&B, at a time when the system wasn't ready to receive it."
* **Billboard Korea** noted that the title track LIKE I DO showed "a melodic maturity beyond what the idol system typically produces," but lacked the marketing support to break out more widely.
* J-pop and Thai media tended to cover it as an idol feature track rather than a solo debut.

* **Resurgence (2022–2025):** Since James released SOFTEST TOUCH, listeners have rediscovered LIKE I DO as an "overlooked starting point." The 2023–2024 TikTok wave drove the title track's streams up 180%+ in 90 days, while CALL ME went viral through trending audio in the aesthetic R&B space, pushing the EP's combined streams past 210M+ in 2024.


### Accolades

The album received no official award nominations in its release year, but "LIKE I DO (feat. Mark)" surpassed 100 million cumulative streams following the release of its music video in 2022, and several tracks from the album were pulled back into curated R&B and indie-pop playlists between 2019 and 2025.


### Legacy

* LIKE I DO was initially viewed as an experimental soft-launch project for exploring James Hwang's role as a songwriter-led artist within the SM Entertainment system, and as the starting point of the "GONUNMA" persona as his creative alter ego. However, the album's significance grew considerably in the early 2020s as several of its tracks were rediscovered through short-form video platforms, especially TikTok
during 2023–2024.
* "LIKE I DO" became the most notable of these organic viral resurgences, with many younger listeners expressing surprise at the maturity of the melodic structure and emotional phrasing of a song written back in 2016, leading to a retrospective reading of the track as an early indicator of James's signature songwriting identity ahead of his rise to mainstream global recognition in the PULSE era
around the same time.
* "CALL ME" became one of the slow-comfort tracks widely used in motivational and reflective content on TikTok, while "EVERLASTING" gained strong popularity in Thailand and Southeast Asia thanks to a melodic nostalgia in the millennial-era soul pop tone that resonated with the sonic revival of post-pandemic listening culture
in addition.
* "CAN YOU LOVE ME TONIGHT" gained popularity through groove-driven short-form edits, and "AT THE END OF THE NIGHT" was noted within the K-R&B listener community as one of the slow-jam structures that diverged from SM Entertainment's production approach at the time, showcasing James's independent tonal direction since early in his career.
* As a result of more than half the tracklist gaining renewed visibility through social video platforms, LIKE I DO has been reevaluated within the retrospective canon as a foundational document of James Hwang's musical identity, rather than merely the trainee-era experimentation project it was initially seen as.
This rediscovery phenomenon also played a key role in expanding his global audience in late 2023 through early 2024, coinciding with the success of "HOW DOES IT FEEL?" from the album PULSE, and contributed directly to the commercial momentum and cultural anticipation ahead of the release of his next album, Are You Still (2024), which became the peak-phase milestone of his career trajectory in the early-solo era.

### Additional Notes

* The tracklist is sequenced as a linear narrative: falling in love → confession → after the breakup → discovering her infidelity (YOU KNOW at track 5) → still wanting her back → drowning in that night — opening with a high bounce that gradually deepens and darkens, closing on a late-night intimacy
* This project served as a creative soft launch for the artist within the SM Entertainment system
* It was later seen as an early blueprint for James Hwang's future musical direction, and was rediscovered among R&B/indie listeners between 2019 and 2025
