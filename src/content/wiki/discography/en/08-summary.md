# GONUNMA (James Hwang) — Discography Summary

---

## 1. LIKE I DO (2016)
**Digital Project Mini Album** · SM Entertainment  
**Genre:** Contemporary R&B / Alt R&B / Indie Pop / Soul Pop / Bedroom Pop  
**Executive Producer:** Lee Soo-man

### Tracklist
| # | Song | Genre | Speed | Mood & Tone | Hook Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BESTFRIEND | Contemporary R&B / R&Bounce | Fast, energetic | Bold, drunk, passionate | Confessing with liquor courage that he wants her to be more than a friend |
| 2 | LIKE I DO | R&B / Pop / Romantic Soul | Mid-tempo, groovy | Confident, luxurious, pleading | "No one can love you like I do" |
| 3 | EVERLASTING | Acoustic R&B / Soul Pop | Slow-mid | Bold, sincere, romantic | Confessing love to a close friend — "I want you to be my everlasting love" |
| 4 | CALL ME | Bedroom Pop / Indie Pop / Chillout | Slow, late-night | Lonely, kind, still caring | After a breakup, telling her "call me if you get lonely" |
| 5 | YOU KNOW | Contemporary R&B / Indie Pop | Fast, tense | Suspicious, hurt, blunt | "You already know" what you did — a song about infidelity |
| 6 | CAN YOU LOVE ME TONIGHT | Alt R&B / Emotional Pop | Slow, intimate | Pleading, hurt, needy | Asking for a chance to reconcile — "can you love me tonight?" |
| 7 | AT THE END OF THE NIGHT | Smooth R&B / Slow Jam | Slow | Soft, dark, late-night | — |

### Arc
Opens with high bounce → gradually deepens and darkens → closes with late-night intimacy  
YOU KNOW was moved to track 5 so the narrative runs in a straight line: love → confession → after the breakup → discovering her infidelity → still wanting her back → drowning in that night

**Genre:** R&B / Synth Funk / Dance-Pop / Indie Soul / Bedroom Pop  
**Executive Producer:** Lee Soo-man

### Tracklist
| # | Song | Genre | Speed | Mood & Tone | Hook Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | YOU AIN'T GOTTA | Soul / Funk / R&B | Slow-mid, chill | Gentle, accepting, comforting | "You already have everything, you don't need to try for me" |

| # | Song | Genre | Speed | Mood & Tone | Hook Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. ARE U STILL (2024)
**2nd Studio Album** · J.H Company / Warner Music Korea  
**Genre:** Neo-Soul / Funk / Disco-Soul / Contemporary R&B  
**Produced by:** GONUNMA · **Arranged by:** GONUNMA, Sam Wills, Mack Keane

### Tracklist
| # | Song | Genre | Speed | Mood & Tone | Hook Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SLEEPWALKING | Neo-Soul / R&B | Mid-fast | Dreamy, feverish, floaty | Sleepwalking/dreaming of her — a burning desire that can't be stopped |
| 2 | TEARS ON A PRETTY FACE | Contemporary R&B / Synth Funk / Nu-Disco | Fairly fast, bittersweet | Bold, hurt, but still moving forward | Waited for love all along, but it's time to move on — "no tears for someone who doesn't deserve them" |
| 3 | IF I KNEW | Alternative R&B / Soul | Slow-mid | Regretful, longing, remorseful | "If I knew, I wouldn't have let you go" *(pre-release single)* |
| 4 | JUST A DREAM | Neo Soul / Contemporary R&B | Slow, hazy | Alluring, dark-sweet, mysterious | "Slowly, we'll fall in love" — dreamlike |
| 5 | PURPOSE | Alternative R&B / Soul | Mid-tempo | Bitter, confused, trapped | A toxic relationship stuck on repeat — "if it doesn't work, why am I still here?" |
| 6 | BREATHE | Funk / Disco-Soul / R&B | Mid-tempo, soft | Close, comforting, intimate | "Just breathe, stay with me, no need to prove anything" *(the album's midpoint turn)* |
| 7 | ORDINARY FEELINGS | Soulful Pop / R&B / 80s Soul | Mid-tempo | Confused, rushed, touching yet anxious | A relationship moving too fast — "just one more day together" |
| 8 | ANOTHER MAN'S LIFE | Alternative R&B / Soul | Fairly fast, groovy | Sad, letting go, graceful | An old love has moved on to a new life with someone else — "don't waste time living in someone else's life" |
| 9 | SELFISH | R&B / Soul / 90s Groove | Slow, weighty | Deeply hurt, grieving, loving | About to lose someone he loves, begging them to stay a little longer *(groovy sound, but the heaviest lyrics on the album)* |
| 10 | SOMEWHERE SOMEHOW | R&B / Soul / Disco | Slow-mid | Somber, hopeful, encouraging | After the loss, walking toward brighter days |
| 11 | OUT HERE | Soul / R&B / Funk-Disco-Pop | Mid-tempo, groovy | Liberated, self-discovering | "I'm out here now" — a closing statement |

### Arc
First half (1–5): groove → hurt → introspective  
**BREATHE (track 6):** the turning point — cathartic release  
Second half (7–11): reflection and letting go → **SELFISH (track 9) gut punch** → recovery and moving forward  
SELFISH is placed at track 9 so SOMEWHERE SOMEHOW and OUT HERE work as "still moving forward after the loss" — reframing the meaning of the two closing tracks to hit harder

## 5. CAN'T LEAVE ALONE (2025)
**3rd Studio Album** · J.H Company / Warner Music Korea  
**Genre:** Contemporary R&B / Pop-R&B / K-R&B / Alternative R&B  
**Produced & Arranged by:** GONUNMA, Pino, JUNNY

### Tracklist
| # | Song | Genre | Speed | Mood & Tone | Hook Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CAN'T LEAVE ALONE (feat. Pharrell Williams) | R&B / Contemporary R&B / Neptunes-style groove | Mid-fast, groovy | Obsessed, magnetically charming | "I can't leave here without you" — utterly captivated, unable to hold back |
| 2 | SOUR | R&B / Pop-R&B / K-R&B | Fast, club-energy | Magnetic, playing hard to get, hot | She's beautiful but plays hard to get — a sour attitude that only makes him want her more |
| 3 | MYSTERY LADY | R&B / Soul | Slow-mid | Mysterious, entranced, untouchable | She's like an illusion — appearing, then vanishing |
| 4 | HONEY | Pop-R&B / 2000s-inspired R&B | Mid-tempo, sweet | Secretive, exciting, celebrity romance | Finally together, sweet as honey — hiding from paparazzi |
| 5 | I WANT YOU BACK (Interlude) | R&B / Soul | — | (Interlude — transition SOUR→HONEY side) | No lyrics — serves as a genre transition between the two halves |
| 6 | HE'S NOT ME | R&B / Soul | Mid-tempo, bouncy | Confident, unassuming, direct | "You know he can't compete with me" — telling her to stop comparing |
| 7 | COME BACK TO ME | R&B / Soul | Slow-mid | Vulnerable, asking for a chance | Asking her to come back — the chorus just repeats "come back to me" |
| 8 | SOMETHING REAL | Alternative R&B / Soul | Mid-tempo | Searching, sincere, wanting | "I want something real, not just a game" |
| 9 | LOVE THIS PAIN AWAY | R&B / Soul (melancholic) | Slow | Accepting, hurt, one last hope | The relationship is falling apart — "who will love this pain away?" |

### Arc
**SOUR side (1–4):** obsession → playing hard to get → untouchable → finally together (in secret)  
**I WANT YOU BACK Interlude:** the turning point — flips the narrative  
**HONEY side (6–9):** confident → asking her back → wanting something real → admitting it's falling apart  
**TAKE YOU TONIGHT (Bonus):** late-night experimental closer — set apart from the main narrative
