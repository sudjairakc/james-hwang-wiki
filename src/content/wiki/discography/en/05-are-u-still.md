## Are U Still - 2nd Album (08/01/2024)

2nd Studio Album by GONUNMA (James Hwang)

### Recording and production

ARE U STILL is the second studio album by GONUNMA (James Hwang), released under J.H Company with international distribution through Warner Music Korea. It launched on January 8, 2024, and stands as the clearest musical phase shift in his career trajectory since going solo.
The album was developed as a live-influenced groove composition, emphasizing
* bass-driven structure
* analog synth layering
* dynamic vocal phrasing
* humanized rhythmic feel

In contrast to the precision-based production of PULSE (2023), James stepped back from his solo-producer level of control on this album and brought in a collaborative soul ecosystem, including
* Kyle Dion
* Leven Kali
* Sam Wills
* Mack Keane

to build a more organic sonic texture connected more closely to the neo-soul and funk lineage.


### Production chronology

**Writing — June to October 2023 · Seoul and on tour**

ARE U STILL began before PULSE was even in stores. The first sketches date to the PULSE promotion cycle in late June 2023 — the announcement went out on 22/06 and James was writing again within the week, in the gaps between concept shoots and the press showcase. He has described the album as the first one he did not plan: PULSE had been assembled over four years in isolation, and this material came out of the opposite condition, written fast while a record he had lived with since 2019 was finally in front of people.

The writing then followed him onto the road. From late July the demos were built in hotel rooms across the Asian leg on a travel rig — a Universal Audio Volt interface, a Nord Stage, and a single condenser — shaped every night by hearing what the touring band did to the PULSE material live. Several demos carry the room tone of the hotels they were cut in, kept deliberately rather than cleaned out.

The bridge single "IF I KNEW" was finished first and released on 06/10/2023, three months ahead of the album, to establish the tonal shift before the full record landed.

**Recording — October to early December 2023 · Seoul and Los Angeles**

Where PULSE was cut to a grid in rented rooms, this album was tracked to a room — and for the first time, partly to his own. **JH Studio** in Seongsu-dong opened in October 2023, built with receipts from the first leg of the PULSE tour, and ARE U STILL is the first album recorded there.

| Stage | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Demo consolidation and writing sessions | JH Studio, Seongsu-dong, Seoul | 10/2023, the room's first booking; the collaborative circle flew to Seoul rather than the other way round |
| Band tracking | EastWest Studios, Studio 2, Los Angeles | Eleven days, full rhythm section tracked live to a room mic pair alongside close mics |
| Vocals | JH Studio, Seongsu-dong | Cut against the live band takes rather than to a click, which is why the phrasing sits behind the beat across the record |
| Additional keys and synth | Leven Kali's private room, Inglewood | Analog synth layering — Juno-106, Rhodes Mk I, Wurlitzer 200A |
| Mixing | Larrabee Studios, North Hollywood | Mixed by Manny Marroquin over nine days |
| Mastering | Sterling Sound, Edgewater NJ | Cut by Chris Gehringer |

The collaborative circle — **Kyle Dion**, **Leven Kali**, **Sam Wills**, and **Mack Keane** — was assembled through a single introduction from Kyle Dion in August 2023, and all four worked in the room rather than sending files. This is the first and so far only James Hwang album where outside writers hold a topline credit: on PULSE every melody was his, and here the writing sessions were genuinely shared.

**Publishing and rights — October to December 2023**

- **10/2023** — the Warner Chappell Korea administration deal is extended to the second album on identical terms: collection and accounting only, no approval right over content or release
- **11/2023** — split sheets signed in the room at the end of each session rather than negotiated afterwards, a policy James introduced after watching the SM catalog dispute turn on undocumented contributions
- **12/2023** — US session-musician agreements filed under AFM Sound Recording Labor Agreement scale, the company's first American union payroll

**Title track selection — 20/12/2023 to 27/12/2023**

The JH Company A&R lead favoured "Breathe" — the most immediate, most obviously radio-shaped track on the album, and the easiest campaign to run. James overruled it. The album's thesis was the gap between how something looks and how it feels, and leading with the groove record would have sold the surface. "Breathe" was held back and released as the second single a month later, which is why it has its own music video despite not being a title track.

Because there is no label A&R above the company, the decision took one meeting and no escalation — a process James has contrasted directly with the six-week title-track committee cycles of his SM years.

**Choreography and staging — 15/12/2023 to 05/01/2024**

Because ARE U STILL is a groove album rather than a performance album, the choreography brief inverted the PULSE approach: instead of eight dancers in formation, James asked for a two-dancer setup that could hold an entire stage with movement built around weight-shift rather than sync.

- Commissioned from **Kim Ye-rim** and the **YGX** creative team, Gangnam-gu
- **15/12 – 26/12/2023** — creation block at YGX
- **27/12/2023 – 05/01/2024** — rehearsal at CJ Rehearsal Studio, Sangam-dong, running alongside PULSE 2024 tour rehearsals in the same building

### Music and lyrics

Musically, ARE U STILL shifts direction from the hybrid R&B of the previous album toward
* Neo-Soul
* Funk
* Disco-Soul
* Contemporary R&B

drawing clear influence from the musical grammar of Michael Jackson across groove structure, vocal phrasing, and rhythmic staging. <br />
The album opener "Sleepwalking" uses a choreography concept referencing the movement language of late-70s/early-80s funk-disco, with a retro-soul performance video visual tone that serves as a transition gateway into the album's new aesthetic. <br />
The title track "Tears On A Pretty Face" is a synth-funk / nu-disco performance single widely discussed for its blend of
* MJ-style vocal phrasing
* Bad-era groove attitude
* The Way You Make Me Feel-style staging energy
* Prince-influenced irony tone

paired with a concept visual featuring a female-only multinational dance ensemble that reflects thematic framing around emotional autonomy and a feminist undertone. <br />
"If I Knew" was previously released during the PULSE Tour (2023) and serves as a bridge track connecting the sonic language between the two eras. <br />
"Just A Dream" (feat. Janet Jackson) is a neo-soul duet noted as a legacy-lineage collaboration between a contemporary R&B artist and Janet Jackson, and serves as a pivot track into the album's mid-album soul section. <br />
"Purpose" and "Selfish" show 1990s R&B-soul influence and act as tonal stabilizers within the album's structure. <br />
"Breathe" (track 6) is a groove-driven disco-soul performance track compared to the aesthetic grammar of "Off the Wall"-era funk-pop, functioning as the album's midpoint cathartic release. <br />
"Ordinary Feelings" and "Another Man's Life" emphasize lyric-driven neo-soul storytelling, with vocal phrasing as the core of the tracks' identity. <br />
"Selfish" was deliberately placed at track 9 — a sequencing masterstroke: the sound grooves like 90s soul, but the content carries the heaviest loss on the album. This placement reframes the two closing tracks to function as "life goes on after loss." <br />
The two closing tracks, "Somewhere, Somehow" and "Out Here," use abstract lyric structures and floating harmonic progressions to build the album's reflective closing arc.

### Marketing

Although ARE U STILL was a significant stylistic pivot album, its rollout was under-promoted by design, as the artist moved directly into a continuous concert tour cycle immediately after release. <br />
* The promotional strategy included
* a concept video for "Sleepwalking"
* a music video for "Tears On A Pretty Face"
* a performance-based visual rollout for "Breathe"
* a pre-release single strategy ("If I Knew")

Despite the lack of extended broadcast promotion, reception within the international R&B market and producer community gave the album a sustained long-tail reception throughout 2024.


### Critical Reception

ARE U STILL is a career-peak moment both critically and commercially, widely recognized as the album that confirmed James Hwang's status as a genuine global R&B artist. The album received an average score of **88/100** from aggregators — the highest of any release in his career to date.

It has been described as <br />
_"his most musically ambitious work to date"_
and <br />
_"a deliberate pivot toward legacy-driven sound rather than trend-driven production"_ <br />
Several reviews noted that the album's songwriting maturity outpaced the expectations of some fans, causing reception within the fanbase to split into two camps.
However, among international critics, the album was praised as <br />
**one of the strongest Asian-led neo-soul projects of the decade**

Critical response:
* **Pitchfork** (Best New Music, score 8.4) wrote, "ARE U STILL is proof that James Hwang isn't just a K-pop artist doing R&B — he's an R&B artist who happens to be from Korea. TEARS ON A PRETTY FACE is one of the best vocal performances of 2024."
* **Rolling Stone** named it "Best R&B Album of 2024 (International)" and described JUST A DREAM feat. Janet Jackson as "not just a feature done for credibility, but a conversation between two eras that unfolds naturally."
* **NME** gave it 4.5/5 and called it "the moment K-R&B stopped being a genre qualifier and became just R&B."
* **IZM (Korea)** gave it 4.5/5 and noted that "the soul-disco hybrid structure in BREATHE and SOMEWHERE SOMEHOW shows that this artist has far deeper musical literacy than expected."
* **Grammy Committee (64th Grammy Awards):** the album earned James his first-ever consideration in the Best Progressive R&B Album category.
* **User & chart reception:** Melon #2 within the first 3 days, Circle Digital Top 5 for 6 consecutive weeks, over 2.2B+ total streams to date, and a 9.4/10 user review score on Melon.

### Legacy

ARE U STILL is viewed as the album that confirmed James Hwang's status as a
lineage-aligned neo-soul artist within the global R&B ecosystem
and marks a turning point from <br />
_**producer-driven independence (PULSE)**_
toward a
_**legacy-rooted artistic identity phase**_ <br />
Although the album had limited broadcast promotion, reception within the international market and industry community gave it sustained recognition throughout 2024. <br />
In addition, the fact that the artist released only one album that year before moving into a concert tour cycle and a continuous run of film activities — along with returning to activities with NCT 127 later in the year — gave the album a long-tail cultural presence throughout the year. <br />
In terms of discography development, ARE U STILL is often cited as
the defining pivot album between independence and legacy alignment in James Hwang's solo career
