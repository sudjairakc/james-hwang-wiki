## SOFTEST TOUCH - 1st Mini Album (11/01/2022)

### Recording and production

SOFTEST TOUCH was recorded between February 2016 and December 2019 within SM Entertainment's production system and international collaborators, marking GONUNMA (James Hwang)'s first official solo mini album. <br />
The album was developed as an exception case within SM Entertainment's solo release sequencing system, with the project fast-tracked following the global market response to his previous R&B work, including activities through SM Station during 2018–2019. <br />
James served as topliner, lyricist, co-producer, and concept lead for the entire album, under the supervision of senior producers Ryan S. Jhun, Kenzie, and Yoo Young-jin, to maintain sonic consistency in line with the label's standards. <br />
Executive production was overseen by Lee Soo-man


### Music and lyrics

Musically, SOFTEST TOUCH expands the sonic palette from the intimate R&B of LIKE I DO (2016) toward hybrid R&B, synth funk, indie soul, and dance-pop with a more global orientation.
The lyrics retain the same core thematic threads, including:
* vulnerability in relationships
* emotional intimacy
* late-night reflection
* identity negotiation as a solo artist

However, this body of work shows growth in production scale and rhythmic confidence, particularly on the title track “SOFTEST TOUCH,” which blends a synth funk groove with modern R&B phrasing. <br />
The songs “YOU AIN’T GOTTA” and “CRUSH” were included on the album to connect the narrative from his earlier SM Station activities.


### Marketing

SOFTEST TOUCH is James Hwang’s official solo debut, released as both a physical and digital release under SM Entertainment. <br />
Promotion included:
* a music video for the title track
* live performance content
* digital campaign targeting global R&B listeners
* curated playlist placements in Asian and North American markets
The album was presented as a
transition project from idol songwriter to an artist-led solo identity
and was positioned clearly apart from his activities within NCT 127.


### Critical reception

SOFTEST TOUCH received a positive reception from critics and listeners both in Korea and abroad, particularly within R&B and alternative K-pop circles. It is regarded as a solo debut that clearly demonstrated artistic courage in stepping outside the SM system.

Critical response:
* **IZM (Korea)** gave the album a 4.0/5 rating and wrote, “SOFTEST TOUCH is the clearest evidence that James Hwang is not an idol waiting for the system to define him, but an artist who has always known himself — he just needed the space.”
* **Pitchfork** (Seoul section) described it as “a mini album with unusually high emotional directness for a Korean artist’s solo R&B debut, especially NO RESERVATION, which features a vocal precision rarely found in this era.”
* **Consequence of Sound** praised CRUSH feat. Jungkook as “the most organic K-pop crossover of 2022 — without even a hint of marketing artifice.”
* **The Korea Herald** stated that the album meant “the definition of a K-pop idol had to be redefined once again.”

* **Commercial reception:** CRUSH feat. Jungkook became a breakout track with 260M+ streams on Spotify, the highest in his catalog at the time, propelling the EP into the Circle Album Top 5 and building a significant fanbase in the North American and Southeast Asian markets.


### Accolades

Although the album was not positioned as an awards-driven release, the title track “SOFTEST TOUCH” received a strong response on streaming platforms and in international playlist circulation following its release.
“LIKE I DO” (from his previous project) and “CRUSH” received renewed attention alongside the album’s release, driving a sustained streaming uplift across his earlier catalog.
The album was also featured on curated playlists across
* global R&B
* indie soul
* alternative K-R&B in multiple markets


### Legacy

SOFTEST TOUCH stands as the solo debut that confirmed James Hwang’s status as a songwriter-led solo artist within the SM Entertainment system.
The album:
connects the narrative from the SM Station period (2018–2019)
expands the sonic identity established in LIKE I DO
and builds the framework for his later solo work
Within the industry, the album is regarded as one of the rare cases of an SM artist holding a high degree of creative control from the solo debut stage onward.
