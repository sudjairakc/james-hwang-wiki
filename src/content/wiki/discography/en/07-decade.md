## DECADE - 1st Anthology Album (09/09/2026)

1st Anthology Album by GONUNMA (James Hwang)

### Recording and production

DECADE is GONUNMA's (James Hwang) first anthology album, released on September 9, 2026 under J.H Company with international distribution by Warner Music Korea, marking the 10th anniversary of his debut as a member of NCT 127 in 2016 <br />
The album serves as both a retrospective collection and a curated artistic timeline, gathering work from every major chapter of his discography from LIKE I DO (2016) through CAN'T LEAVE ALONE (2025), alongside 4 new songs that function as a transition bridge into the next creative phase <br />
Beyond the new songs, the album also includes
* solo reinterpretation versions
* live rearranged versions from the PULSE Tour
* archival spoken material from recording sessions across 2015–2025

which reflects the artist's development in vocal delivery and production philosophy over the 10-year span
The entire album is produced by GONUNMA


### Music and lyrics

Musically, DECADE spans the main styles James has explored throughout his career trajectory, including
* Contemporary R&B
* Pop-R&B
* Soul
* Funk
* Indie Pop

The anthology's structure is divided into two parts
* **Disk 1 - Identity & Impact**: collects songs that served as turning points in each era, along with performance-defining singles
* **Disk 2 - Process & Emotion**: collects deep cuts, songwriting-driven material, and reinterpretations that reflect the artist's emotional authorship

The 4 new songs on the album maintain the sonic direction rooted in Contemporary R&B and the melodic minimalism of GONUNMA's mature era, and have been noted as a quality continuation from the CAN'T LEAVE ALONE period  <br />
The anthology also includes live rearranged versions of
* “RIGHT ABOUT LOVE (Extended Live Version)”
* “NO RESERVATION (Extended Live Version)”

developed during the PULSE Tour (2023), which received strong reception from listeners as versions that expand the dynamic range and harmonic layering of the originals


### Archival spoken material

One of the key elements of DECADE is the inclusion of liner-note essay recordings and studio fragments from the making of previous albums <br />
This material includes
* demo narration segments
* melodic sketch recordings
* spoken reflections during recording sessions
* transitional voice memos that connect each era of the discography
* the archival material is sequenced to function as
a sonic documentary layer for the anthology
and reflects James's development from an idol-system songwriter to a fully creative-autonomous artist-producer


### Marketing

DECADE is James's first anthology release to fully return to a physical collector format (2CD) since the period before PULSE (2023) <br />
The physical edition was designed as an archival object for long-time fans, rather than as a streaming-first release <br />
Album promotion included
* a global digital rollout through the Warner network
* live stage performances in South Korea for one of the anthology's new songs
* an anniversary campaign celebrating the 10-year career milestone

However, the album was not promoted in Thailand at the artist's request, and no interviews or broadcast promotion took place domestically during the same period


### Critical Reception

Following its release, DECADE received widespread positive reception as a curator-driven retrospective rather than a label-assembled compilation. The album earned an average score of **84/100** from aggregators, higher than typically expected for an anthology release

Critical response:
* **Pitchfork** (score 8.0) wrote: "DECADE isn't just a greatest hits collection — it's a self-portrait where the artist chose every brushstroke himself. The archival spoken material layers make this anthology sound more like a memoir than a music collection."
* **The Guardian** gave 4/5 and stated: "James Hwang has used his 10th anniversary to create the best entry in this genre since Beyoncé's 4Ever — sequencing that reflects genuine emotional intelligence."
* **Billboard Korea** described: "All 4 new songs, especially NEW, function as a declaration of intent toward the next chapter, rather than mere album filler."
* **IZM (Korea)** gave 4.5/5 and noted: "The live rearranged versions of RIGHT ABOUT LOVE and NO RESERVATION are highlights that prove his studio work surpasses the originals in several dimensions."
* **Complex Korea** called DECADE "the definitive argument for GONUNMA's place in the global R&B conversation — not just in Asia"

**Catalog impact:** Within 30 days of DECADE's release, overall back-catalog streams increased by an average of 22–41% across all eras, with LIKE I DO (+41%), SOFTEST TOUCH (+35%), and CRUSH feat. Jungkook (+28%) seeing the highest uplift


### Accolades

**2026**

* Circle Chart Music Awards — Global Artist Recognition (Win)

The album also received editorial recognition in multiple markets as
one of the most cohesive career anthologies by an Asian R&B solo artist of the decade


### Legacy

DECADE is viewed as an anthology that serves as a
canonization release for the first 10 years of GONUNMA's discography  <br />
The album was not designed as a traditional greatest hits collection, but rather as a curated narrative timeline reflecting the artist's evolution from
idol-system songwriter→ independent producer-artist→ global R&B lineage-aligned soloist  <br />
The inclusion of live rearranged material and archival studio recordings has led the album to be regarded as one of the anthology releases with the highest level of artistic authorship among K-R&B artists of his generation
