## PULSE - 1st Album (21/07/2023)

1st Studio Album by GONUNMA (James Hwang)

### Recording and production

PULSE is GONUNMA's (James Hwang) first full-length studio album, and his first release since founding J.H Company and entering a partnership with Warner Music Korea. It was recorded between January–December 2022 and reworked in early 2023 to restructure the material for a global-level performance rollout.
The album's production process was clearly an artist-driven production workflow — James built every demo himself (melody, topline, and rough arrangement) before bringing in outside collaborators for texture enhancement, then re-arranged the tracks himself to lock in the final sonic direction.
The album's production credits therefore reflect the model of
artist-controlled production with external texture collaborators
and mark the first release on which he personally controlled the arrangement of every track on the album.


### Music and lyrics

Musically, PULSE blends Contemporary R&B, Synth Funk, Dance-Pop, Indie Pop, and Bedroom Pop, expanding the sonic scale of SOFTEST TOUCH into a structure that is more performance-oriented and globally accessible.
Notable features of the album include
* loop-based composition structure
* minimal drum programming
* layered harmony stacks
* falsetto-driven topline phrasing
* groove-first arrangement design

The album maintains thematic continuity with previous work, including
* emotional ambiguity
* intimacy after a relationship
* identity transition after leaving the label system
but adds greater tonal contrast within the tracklist to build a performance catalog structure rather than the traditional narrative-linear album. <br />

Examples of each track's tonal reception:
* HOW DOES IT FEEL?, the title track, is clearly influenced by Justin Timberlake-style groove-driven pop-funk, standing out with its falsetto hook and stage-oriented rhythm structure. It was praised as one of the most "ahead-of-its-time" performance singles of 2023 and proved highly popular among fans.
* SAY (feat. keshi) has been cited as one of the album's most significant unexpected collaborations — its dual-vocal texture, pairing Korean pop phrasing with keshi's alternative R&B, functions as the release's main global positioning track.
* ALL FOR YOU drew interest from R&B listeners for its clearly 90s-style R&B chord progressions and mid-tempo groove that overtly references the genre's classic era.
* TALK TO ME is one of the album's key tonal-contrast tracks, its dark alternative R&B atmosphere drawing comparisons to Taemin's mood design; while praised for its aesthetic, some fans felt it clearly departed from the album's main tone.
* SAVE ME and AWKWARD MAYBE have American radio-friendly melodic pop structures and are seen as the album's accessibility anchors, balancing experimental R&B against a global pop listening base.
* CHEEKY carries an indie-alternative tone close to the SOFTEST TOUCH era, earning a warm response from longtime listeners even though some felt it diverged from the album's main thematic direction.
* IF is a light pop-ballad transition track that delivers a tonal surprise late in the album.
* OUTRO: GIRLFRIEND is a closing track built on a melodic-loop structure centered on lyrical repetition; despite its romantic minimalism, it was praised for its beat construction and its function as an album closer.
* CHICKEN THIGNS (CHEEKY Alt.) is a bonus acoustic demo reinterpretation of "CHEEKY" that reworks the lyrical motif into a playful parody version, drawing fan interest as an alternate studio sketch that reflects the songwriting process.


### Critical Reception

PULSE received a good-to-very-good response from critics, and was seen as proof that James Hwang could exist as a full-scale solo artist who freely controls both creative direction and commercial strategy. The album scored an average of **82/100** on a Korean critics aggregator.

Critical response:
* **Melon Critics** described HOW DOES IT FEEL? as "the most powerful performance single in K-R&B in 2023 — every frame of the stage belongs entirely to him."
* **Billboard Korea** named SAY feat. keshi the "best K-pop crossover collaboration of 2023," calling its dual-vocal structure a new blueprint for international R&B positioning.
* **Pitchfork** (Best New Music, score 7.8) wrote, "PULSE sounds like an artist who can stand on his own — the grooves carry clear intention, and his falsetto hook does heavy lifting on every track."
* **Complex** stated, "If you need proof that K-R&B can compete with Western R&B at the production level, PULSE is the answer."
* **User & chart reception:** Melon #7, Circle Digital Top 15 in its first week, with a 9.1/10 score on the Melon user review aggregator. HOW DOES IT FEEL? was featured on the Spotify Editorial Playlists "R&B Central" and "K-Pop Rising" simultaneously in its first week of release.

### Marketing

PULSE used a multi-track promotion strategy rather than the traditional single rollout model.
Promotion included
* music videos
* dance session videos
* performance clips
* playlist-based targeting
with the following tracks serving as performance anchors
* HOW DOES IT FEEL?
* SAY
* ALL FOR YOU
* TALK TO ME
The album received an international rollout through Warner M's network, departing from the traditional narrative-driven K-pop studio album structure.

### Legacy

PULSE is regarded as the album that most completely shifted James Hwang's status from
SM-system soloist → global producer-artist
in his entire discography.
This album
was the first release under J.H Company
was a fully self-arranged album
used the Warner international rollout system
and set the template for performance-based album promotion in his subsequent work.
Within the industry, PULSE is often cited as one of the artist-produced Korean solo albums with the highest global playlist penetration of 2023, and as the turning-point release of his career phase after leaving SM Entertainment.

### Additional Notes

* The album used a multi-track promotion strategy — it did not follow the traditional single structure.
* MVs, Dance Sessions, and Performance Videos were released for multiple tracks (e.g. HOW DOES IT FEEL?, SAY, ALL FOR YOU, TALK TO ME), meaning "almost every track could function as a promotional song."
* This reflects the fact that the album was designed as a performance catalog, not just an ordinary tracklist.
* It marks a key turning point: from "solo artist within the system" → "producer-artist who controls both the sound and the rollout strategy."
