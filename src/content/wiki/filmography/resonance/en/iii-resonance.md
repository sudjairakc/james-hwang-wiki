## ACT I — 5 Years of Silence and Waiting

A shattered life: Five years have passed. The universe now lies under the grip of the High Cantor, who has swept away every note of music, leaving only emptiness behind.

Warriors in black: Mina brought Vale and Lyra to safety. They became a band of shadow rebels striking from the fringes of the universe. Vale grew into a sharp, silent marksman, while Lyra honed her powers through instinct until she became formidably strong.

Heart of the machine: Aboard the hidden RESONANCE ship, Elias — still deaf — spent years training alongside Koro and Nova until he could feel the rhythm of the universe through the vibrations in his feet and skin. He didn't wait for his hearing to return; instead, he learned to "become one with the rhythm," emerging as the most decisive and powerful warrior there had ever been.

## ACT II — Orpheus's Secret Plan and the Reunion

A crack in the darkness: Orpheus was forced to serve the High Cantor as overseer of the Void system, but out of bitter resentment, he secretly planted a "frequency blind spot" at the heart of the enemy's weapon, waiting for the day Elias would return.

A long-awaited reunion: Elias led the RESONANCE ship in a raid to rescue Orpheus, and reunited with Mina, Vale, and Lyra after years apart. The look in everyone's eyes had changed — no longer the naive young people they once were, but battle-hardened warriors who had been through countless campaigns.

## ACT III — The Secret in the Melody (The Missing Note)

A hidden legacy: Orpheus reveals a truth that stuns everyone — the Beatbox melody Elias has played his whole life since childhood is, in fact, an "ultimate sound code" that Orpheus embedded in his subconscious to shut down the High Cantor's system in the final moment.

The key in his breath: Elias realizes he has carried the key to saving the universe with him all along. Every time he hummed that tune, he was unknowingly preparing for this very war.

## ACT IV — The Decisive Battle at the Heart of the Void

The final great battle: The seven members of Team Resonance break through into the heart of the High Cantor's stronghold amid a fierce barrage of fire. Vale and Lyra guard the path with unmatched skill.

The end of the silence: Elias confronts the High Cantor in an airless hall. He uses no words — only a Beatbox carrying the ultimate power of Resonance, combined with the code Orpheus had planted. An invisible soundwave slams into the High Cantor, tearing its system apart in agony, decisively closing the chapter on a long, bitter vendetta.

## ACT V — Epilogue: A Melody of Farewell

After the victory, everyone returns to their home planet, Melodia, now beginning to recover, amid bonfires and the wreckage of ruined ships.

Vale & Lyra affirm that they will retire here to build a family, weary of fighting and longing for a peaceful life.

Koro & Nova bicker over the mangled spare parts needed to repair the RESONANCE ship. Koro decides to set up a garage and a mechanics' school here, with Nova as his lifelong bickering partner.

Orpheus chooses to stay and atone by teaching music and recording history on this planet.

Elias & Mina: Elias still burns with the fire of exploration. He announces he will go on to listen to the music of other worlds, and Mina nods, agreeing to travel with him as his "ears" and "guide."

The closing scene shows the warmth of the group's parting. Friends stand seeing off Elias and Mina in front of their small ship. Koro shouts a last complaint about engine oil, Vale gives a military salute, and Nova projects a holographic team emblem across the sky. Elias starts the engine and launches toward the stars, leaving behind a simple, freedom-filled Beatbox rhythm as his farewell. The legend of Resonance comes to an end.

---

After the film's release, James Hwang gave an interview confirming that the narrative arc of Elias and the Starwave crew was designed from the outset to be a complete saga, considered fully closed with this trilogy. He noted that the Resonance universe would continue to be developed through other projects in the future, with him remaining involved as the creative architect of that universe.
