🚀 THE STARWAVE CREW
1. EO (The Reluctant Conductor)
* Concept: From a lonely scavenger to a captain who carries the rhythm of the galaxy on his shoulders.
* Design: A lean young man with slightly dark skin, a face that reads gentle but with sadness hidden behind his eyes.
* Outfit: Wears an old canvas jacket patched with heatsinks along the elbows, but has a single armored glove made of chrome, and a silver mask that reflects an iridescent shimmer when struck by sound.
* Equipment: "The Conductor's Glove" — a glove used to tune frequencies and snap his fingers to send out Force Resonance signals.
* Key Trait: He doesn't just hear speech — he hears the "Life Melody" of the people around him.
* Casting: James Hwang
    * Why him: He's a rising star with overflowing charisma, and crucially, he's a genuine singer/musician. Hwang has the soft, artistic quality that EO needs, and he can carry quiet dramatic scenes well.
    * Vibe: A scavenger kid who looks "clean" and always carries hope in his eyes.
2. LYRA VENN (The Broken Harmony)
* Concept: A wandering former Jedi trying to find meaning in stillness within a deafening universe.
* Design: A graceful young woman with short, glassy silver hair, and clear blue eyes that always look distant.
* Outfit: A Jedi-like robe, but tailored from tattered native fabric, wearing a single shoulder armor plate with its insignia scraped off.
* Ability: "Sonic Force Pulse" — she doesn't wield a lightsaber; instead she uses the Force to control the vibration of air molecules (creating sound walls or shockwaves).
* Key Trait: She often wears holographic headphones to "filter" the universe's cries of pain from overwhelming her mind.
* Casting: Cailee Spaeny (from Priscilla / Alien: Romulus)
    * Why her: She's the rising queen of new-generation sci-fi. Cailee has a face that looks "Fragile" yet carries hidden resolve — a perfect fit for a Jedi who has lost her faith but still wants to protect people. Her price point is still within reach for a tentpole series.
    * Vibe: Small but packing enormous hidden power.
3. VALE RENN (The Percussion of War)
* Concept: An abandoned special forces soldier who believes in tangible truth more than miracles.
* Design: A middle-aged man, sturdily built, with a scar running across the left side of his face, and a military crew cut.
* Outfit: Dark gray Heavy Tactical armor scorched with laser burns, wearing a metal armband that holds special ammunition.
* Equipment: "The Pulse Breaker" — a heavy machine gun that can tune its firing rhythm to match the beat set by EO, increasing its penetrating power.
* Key Trait: The only one who always pulls the team back to "reality."
* Casting: Damson Idris (from Snowfall)
    * Why him: Damson has a "Quiet Intensity" acting power that suits a special forces soldier who has been through the battlefield and no longer believes in miracles. He can convincingly portray tactical leadership, wariness toward the power of the Force, and the emotional burden of a man abandoned by the system. At the same time, he has enough emotional nuance to naturally develop a slow-building relationship arc with Lyra.
    * Vibe: A frontline soldier who is the "main beat of the battlefield" — hard as steel on the outside, but always listening to the team on the inside.
4. KORO (The Galactic DJ)
* Concept: A mechanical genius who sees every machine as a musical instrument.
* Design: A small alien with yellow-orange skin and four arms (allowing him to repair equipment and adjust the mixer at the same time), wearing large wind goggles.
* Outfit: A mechanic's outfit full of pockets for wrenches and wires, wearing a knit cap with a signal-receiving antenna.
* Equipment: "The Star-Mixer" — a ship control panel he modified to "Remix" the ship's energy waves into a sonic weapon.
* Key Trait: Extremely optimistic, and the one who creates a fun atmosphere on the ship.
* Casting: Nat Faxon (from Our Flag Means Death / Disenchantment)
    * Why him: He's a comedy actor with razor-sharp timing. Nat excels at voice acting and performance capture, making a character that's funny yet lovable. He'll make Koro feel like a teammate you'd want to grab a space beer with.
    * Vibe: The ship's resident scene-stealer who turns tense atmospheres into a party.
5. NOVA (The Soul in the Shell)
* Concept: An ancient-model droid who is the sole witness to the legend of the first captain.
* Design: A spherical droid resembling a golf ball, with three chrome spider-like legs, and a large, bright blue eye-lens in the center.
* Function: A high-frequency processing unit, and the "music archive" of the first captain.
* Personality: Curious, honest, and fond of making "Beep-Bop" sounds in beatbox rhythm.
* Casting: Brian Tyree Henry
    * Why him: He has a vocal weight, warmth, and comedic timing that make Nova feel like more than just a funny droid. He can give a character who doesn't speak much emotion, memory, and a genuine bond with the team.
    * Vibe: A seasoned old droid who sounds more like a comrade-in-arms than an assistant.
6. THE OLD CURATOR / ORPHEUS (The Silent Conductor) (Movie 1)
    * Concept: A sage who watches over collapse and waits for the right moment to fix the past.
    * Design: A hunched old man with unusually long, slender fingers, skin wrinkled like old paper, wearing a cloak made from scraps of wire and glass fiber.
* Casting: Jared Harris
    * Why him: He has a sage-like presence, someone who has been through enough collapse to speak little but carry weight — a perfect fit for a watcher who knows that one wrong beat could change the entire universe.
    * Vibe: A guardian of history who looks calm, but seems to carry the full weight of the past on his shoulders.
7. MINA (The Echo Weaver) (Movie 2)
* Concept: A young woman connected to the dimension of echoes (The Ethereal Observer).
* Design: A delicate build, skin so pale it's almost translucent, faint pastel-colored long hair that floats as if she's always underwater, large eyes that seem to see what others cannot.
* Casting: Hunter Schafer (from Euphoria / Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes)
    * Why her: She has a look that's "Out of this world," with a mysterious, alluring charm similar to Luna Lovegood but leaning more sci-fi. Hunter can portray innocence laced with enlightenment beautifully.
    * Vibe: A stray starlight that wandered onto the Starwave, becoming a new inspiration for the captain.


🌑 THE ANTAGONIST
1. SUPREME LEADER (The Silent Queen)
* Concept: Sorrow frozen solid until it became vengeance.
* Design: A young woman with a face pale as marble, faint purple cracks running along her veins beneath her skin, wearing a sharp-spiked steel headpiece.
* Outfit: A long, jet-black evening gown that looks made of "Dark Matter," rippling like smoke at all times.
* Ability: "The Void" — the ability to create a vacuum of sound and emotion; anyone within range of her power feels "empty" until they lose all will.
* Key Trait: She doesn't want to rule the universe — she wants to "stop" the universe so no one else has to suffer from waiting the way she did.
* Casting: Elizabeth Lail (from You / Five Nights at Freddy's)
    * Why her: She has a beauty that reads "Sad & Haunted." Elizabeth can convey an expression that looks empty yet full of pain extremely well, which is essential for a character frozen by vengeance like the Supreme Leader.
    * Vibe: Vengeance wearing the guise of an angel with broken wings.
2. MALOS (The Shattered Maestro) (Movie 1)
* Concept: A once-great conductor who collapsed into becoming a tyrant of silence.
* Design: An elderly man who looks dignified but gaunt, wearing jet-black steel armor covering his chest (to block vibrations), with scars on his face from a past sonic explosion.
* Casting: Claes Bang
    * Why him: He has a cold elegance and a sharp look sharp enough to make Malos feel like a villain with taste, ritual, and psychological fracture — not just an ordinary oppressor.
    * Vibe: A shattered conductor still standing like the final movement before ruin.
3. THE HIGH CANTOR (The Void Architect) (Movie 2)
* Concept: The leader of a cult of emptiness who believes "silence is the only purity."
* Design: An unnaturally tall, elongated figure, wearing matte black textured armor that absorbs all light, with no mouth (communicates through mental vibration).
* Casting: Doug Jones (from Star Trek: Discovery / Pan's Labyrinth)
    * Why him: The master of performance capture and body movement that's both horrifying and elegant. Doug will make this villain feel "unsettling" every time he appears, without saying a single word.
    * Vibe: Silence creeping in like death.

🛸 THE STARWAVE (The Ship)
* Design: A spacecraft shaped like a wedge with smoothly curved wings, looking like a blend between a warship and a piano.
* Feature: Its surface is made of chrome metal that can change color in time with the music (Rhythm-reactive Hull); at high speed it leaves behind long trailing rainbow streaks of light.
