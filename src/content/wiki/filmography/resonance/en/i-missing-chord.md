## ACT 0 — THE BROKEN HARMONY *(The Crack in the Melody)*
### 1. The Melodia Cathedral – 15 Years Ago *(The Intro)*
The sky above the planet Melodia once shone golden, shimmering with "crystal song waves" that sustained all life. Malos, in his younger years, was a Grand Tuner brimming with hope. He was performing the most important ritual of his life: cleansing the spirit of his daughter Myra, who was fading from a condition known as "Corporeal Dissolution."
* The Ritual: Malos cradled the Twin Resonance Forks, representing the heartbeats of father and daughter. He began playing the most delicate melody, and golden sound waves slowly wrapped around Myra's body, drawing her back into physical form.
* The Accident: Just as the two heartbeats were about to merge into one, a war siren from outside blared and disrupted the frequency for a fraction of a second, triggering a violent Feedback Loop.
* The Tragedy: The distorted sound wave turned into a mass-destroying force. The cathedral shook and shattered. Myra's body dissolved into motes of light before Malos's eyes. He survived, but his hearing was destroyed — left with nothing but a constant ringing (tinnitus) that endlessly reminded him of his loss.
Malos (screaming amid the ruins of the cathedral): "Music is poison... sound is a lie! Only silence will never betray anyone!"

### 2. Lotho Minor, the Junk Planet – 3 Months Later *(The Encounter)*
Malos, clad in pitch-black chrome armor built to block out all sound, traveled to the wreckage of the cathedral, which had fallen into a graveyard of space debris. His goal was to find the "Mother Tuning Fork" to build a Silence Generator.
* The Discovery: Amid the frigid piles of scrap metal, he found a small boy, seven years old, curled up and trembling. In the boy's hands, he clutched the "Child Tuning Fork" tightly, as if it were his last refuge.
* The Cruel Mercy: Malos recognized the key instantly — it was the Hardware Key he himself had built, and the last object Myra had touched before she died. His revulsion was so strong he couldn't bear to touch it, not even with a fingertip.
* The Parting Word: He sneered with contempt and decided to leave the "crucial key" in the orphan's hands, believing this junkyard would become a grave that buried both the boy and the key together.
Malos: "Hold on to that garbage, boy... someday the hope in your hands will stab you the same way it did me."

### 3. Aboard the Wrecked Warship – Present Day *(The Quiet Character Beat)*
(Before entering ACT I — to let the audience bond with Nova)
The camera drifts through the wreckage of a warship and settles on the young Captain and his droid companion Nova, sitting together on the edge of the old wreck, gazing out at the Milky Way.
* The Beatbox Lesson: Nova makes clicking sounds, trying to imitate the noise of a broken engine. The young Captain laughs and moves his mouth to beatbox a short rhythm. "Hey, kid, not like that... listen — chick-chk-chick-boom... that's the rhythm of the Starwave."
* The Connection: Nova tries again and nails it perfectly. The young Captain pats the little droid's shoulder fondly. "Nice work... if you weren't around to remember these sounds, this universe would be a lonely place."
* The Calm before the Storm: The two share a quiet moment before returning to the ship, with the young Captain having no idea that the "heartbeat" he taught Nova today would one day become the only thing that could save the world from Malos's silence.

## ACT I — THE ECHOES OF FREEDOM *(The Rhythm of Freedom)*
### 1. The Graveyard of Silence *(The Status Quo)*

The camera opens on a long take, drifting through the wreckage of warships in the "Dead Zone." The Captain, dressed in a scavenger suit and goggles, appears alongside Nova, his loyal droid companion, who is "collecting sounds" to compose a new musical note. Their bond looks warm and close. The Captain pats the little droid affectionately after gathering 88% of the rhythm.
> **[Pacing Note: Quiet Character Beat]** Before moving on, the Captain pauses to watch Nova try to mimic the clang of metal striking metal. The Captain laughs softly and taps back a rhythm in a short code known only to the two of them, establishing that Nova isn't just a robot, but part of his soul.

### 2. Aboard the Starwave — An Interrupted Frequency *(The Signal Interference)*

Back aboard the ship, the smell of engine oil and the liveliness of the Starwave family — Koro, Vale, and Lyra — create a relaxed atmosphere. But the peace is short-lived. While the Captain is mixing the "space junk" sounds he's collected, the ship's communication system is suddenly disrupted by "The Discordant Hum." Monitors across the ship glitch into pitch black. Nova convulses violently, its eyes flashing red for a moment, spewing out ancient code without stopping.

### 3. Remote Assault *(The Remote Hacking)*

Suddenly, an image of Malos appears on the flickering comm screen. He hasn't come to talk — he begins "hacking" Nova remotely. The steel-colored light from Nova's body starts being drained away. The little droid lets out a pitiful, electronic scream.
"You've taken remarkably good care of my antenna, Captain..." Malos says in a cold voice. "I've planted a 'Mass Dissolution Virus' in its core. If you don't return it to the Melodia Cathedral within one hour, this droid will become a sonic bomb that tears your ship to pieces."
Nova goes completely still after Malos finishes speaking, the light in its eyes going dark. The Captain shouts Nova's name at the top of his lungs, but to no avail.

### 4. The Herding Hunters *(The Herding Hunt)*

Before the Captain can even cradle Nova's body, a squadron of a dozen or more Acolytes of Silence emerges from the shadows. They don't open fire to destroy — instead, they surround the ship and fire frequency-pulse rounds, forcing the Starwave to divert onto a single course: toward the planet Melodia.
"They're not letting us run to the New Republic forces!" Vale shouts as he mans the gun turret. "They're clearly herding us into a pen!"
"It wants us to carry Nova's signal to the cathedral, to amplify its power of 'silence' across the galaxy," Lyra says, sensing the dark vibration. "If Nova gets close to that generator, Malos's power will surge to a point nothing can stop."

### 5. A Desperate Gamble *(The Desperate Move)*

The Captain looks at Nova lying motionless, and at the enemy squadron closing in. He decides to launch himself outside the ship to use a Resonance Pulse and blast open a temporary escape route — but it's only buying time.
"Koro! Check Nova's systems right now. We have no choice but to head to Melodia, just as it wants," the Captain declares firmly as he returns to the pilot's seat.
"But that's a trap, Captain! We'd be walking the bait right into the tiger's mouth!" Koro protests.
"I know... but it's underestimating us," the Captain says, gripping the tuning fork hidden inside his jacket.
"It thinks we're just prey struggling to survive, but we're going to walk into its house and tear its system apart from the inside, with a rhythm it never saw coming!"
The Starwave surges into hyperspace, heading for Melodia, with the Acolyte squadron trailing like a shadow. The 60-minute ticking clock begins its final countdown.

## ACT II-A — THE PAST RETURNS *(The Past that Returns)*
### 1. Wounds Beneath the Cipher *(The Ticking Clock)*

The atmosphere aboard the Starwave feels heavy, as if submerged beneath a soundless ocean. Emergency lights flash orange and red against the darkness in a steady rhythm. The digital countdown on the main screen ticks down mercilessly: "00:55:00." Koro sinks down beside the metal console, the face of the once-cheerful engineer now pale.
"Captain... the Mass Dissolution Virus Malos planted is eating away at Nova's main circuitry, burning it to a crisp. If we don't get pure gold ore to make new signal-conducting wiring, even if we get him to the cathedral, he'll be nothing but a hunk of metal waiting to explode." He circles the rhythm while pointing at a complex sound-wave graph.
"And what's worse, the core system is locked behind a 'Voice Print' protocol, as if it's waiting for someone specific whose identity it can truly verify before it grants Admin access... and that's exactly why Malos keeps sending hacking interference into Nova periodically — to force us to rush to his cathedral looking for a fix!"
The Captain looks at the glaring red countdown before turning to give orders in a decisive tone. "We split up here. Vale, Lyra... you head to the Outpost station and steal the gold ore. I'll go down to the Iron Grave alone."

### 2. A Secret Buried in Scrap Metal *(The Scavenger's Memory)*

The Captain steers the ship toward the space junkyard he left behind more than a decade ago. Amid the wreckage of giant Destroyers, split in half like the bones of ancient beasts, the Captain launches himself alone into the vacuum.
He climbs over the familiar scrap metal until he comes to a stop before an ancient stone altar standing tall amid the junk. The moment his feet touch the freezing metal ground, once-hazy memories surge back like an electric shock.
He sees the image of a man in chrome armor walking past him fifteen years ago — a man who sneered with contempt while he was just a slum kid clutching a piece of scrap metal to his chest...
"So it really is you..." the Captain murmurs over the comm, his eyes shifting from doubt to complete understanding. "The man named Malos." He pulls the "crystal tuning fork" (Hardware Key) from the altar; its vibration confirms its original owner's identity with disgusting certainty.
"You left it behind because you were disgusted by your own failure, and you assumed a scavenger kid like me would never understand it... but today, the key you abandoned will become the very thing that traps you in your own hell of silence."
> **[Pacing Note: Malos's Perspective]** At that very moment, Malos mutters to himself while watching the monitor observing the Captain: "Pick it up, boy... I'm too disgusted by that key to send anyone to touch it. But soon, the virus inside that droid will force you to carry the key I despise most, and open the cathedral doors for me with your own hands."

### 3. The Engineer's Confession *(The Mechanic's Vigil)*
After the ship splits apart, an uncomfortable silence blankets the engineering bay. Koro sinks down onto a toolbox beside Nova's motionless body. The red light from the droid's eyes flickers faintly, like a breath about to run out. Koro gently wipes the soot from Nova's metal forehead with a cloth.
"Hey, kid... don't you dare give up yet," Koro mutters, his once-cheerful voice now trembling. "I'm the best engineer in this whole sector, you know that? I've fixed hyperdrives that exploded in my own hands. But this damn virus... it's like it's talking to you. It's locked you away in there, all alone."
Koro plugs a data cable into the holographic display, his fingers flying across the keyboard as he tries to hack into the life-support system. "Strange... this is really strange," he pauses when he sees a string of code flashing in ancient characters, overlaid on a sound-wave graph: "Waiting for the Missing Chord."
"A password, huh? Malos locked you with someone's 'chord'... and it also needs a sound wave carrying a genuine human heartbeat rhythm to unlock the Admin system." Koro lets out a long sigh, staring out at the window into space. "I can't guess what it means, but there's someone I'm sure knows... hang on, Nova. The Captain is going to find you the answer."
> **[Pacing Note: The Radio Connection]** As the Captain climbs across the scrap metal on his way back to the ship, Koro's voice crackles in over the radio: "Captain... I found some kind of clue inside Nova. It's locked with a code called the 'Missing Chord,' and it needs a Voice Print that carries real emotion... I think Malos isn't just trying to hack Nova — it's challenging someone's 'memory.' Be careful out there." Elias freezes. Koro's words make the image of the man in chrome armor from his memory grow even sharper.

## ACT II-B — THE COUNTERPLAN *(The Plan Within a Plan)*
### 4. The Outpost Job: A Pink Storm *(The Heist of Vale & Lyra)*

At the pirate station known as The Outpost, the air reeks of engine oil and cigarette smoke. Vale strides into the bar twirling his twin pistols. "Sorry, boys — we're in a bit of a hurry!" The moment he fires to clear a path, a dozen pirates swarm in. Lyra moves like a water spirit, her lightblade slicing through the air, creating a wall of echoing sound that deflects bullets back at the enemy with pinpoint accuracy.
"Vale! Watch out for that gas tank!" Lyra shouts a warning.
But it's too late... Vale's bullet punches through a tank of Laughing Gas, and it explodes with a boom!
> **[Pacing Note: The A+ Psychological Shift]** Pink smoke billows across the bar, but it isn't just a gas that makes people laugh uncontrollably. The pirates' senses begin to warp along with the frequency resonating in the smoke. The roar of gunfire turns into the bone-chilling laughter of people they once hurt in the past.
The once-vicious pirates start collapsing, writhing on the ground. Some laugh maniacally while tears stream down their faces, as the hallucination drags out "memories they tried to forget" and lays them bare through laughter. Vale himself starts to lose his grip on reality, gritting his teeth to keep from sinking into that hallucinatory laughter as he leaps over the counter to grab the crate of gold ore.
In a dark corner behind the bar, Lyra finds an old man locked in an iron cage. "That's the symbol of the Melodia Cathedral..." the old man mutters when he sees the emblem on her blade, his clouded eyes staring through the pink smoke with fear. Lyra wastes no time — she slices the chains apart. "If you want to live, come with us! We're this planet's only hope!"

### 5. A Plan Within a Plan *(00:15:00)*

The Starwave regroups. Koro snatches the gold ore from Vale's hands and rushes to melt it into a tiny wire. "Just in time, Captain!" As the Starwave is being chased through space by the Acolyte squadron, Koro curses in frustration when he sees the gold in the smelting pot still hasn't changed state.
"Captain! Not enough power! The smelter isn't drawing enough energy to reach peak temperature. If this signal wire isn't done in 5 minutes, Nova is going to become a bomb!"
The Captain grits his teeth before slamming the controls forward. "Pull power from the left shield and all backup engines, and route it to the engineering bay!"
"But Captain! That'll turn us into a sitting target!" Vale shouts as the ship shudders from a near-miss. The instant power is cut from the shield, an explosion booms from outside. The Starwave lurches violently, nearly throwing everyone from their seats.
But in the engineering bay... the golden light from the smelter suddenly flares bright. Koro quickly uses tongs to pick up the freshly formed, red-hot gold wire and plant it into Nova's body right in the middle of the chaotic lurching.
Koro: "Captain! This gold wire can reconnect the burnt-out circuit and get it working again, sure, but it's only a 'bypass' around the point the virus is eating through! It's like slapping a patch on a dam that's about to burst. We've got no more than 15 minutes before the short-circuiting energy melts this patch, and Nova will explode even harder than before!"
Just then, the Captain sets the crystal tuning fork down on the metal conference table. Its ringing sound is oddly clear. The old curator staggers backward until his back hits the ship's wall, his clouded eyes widening in fear as if he's seen a demon. "That's... the cathedral's 'Heartbeat Stopper Key'! Where in hell did you dig that up, boy?"
The Captain looks at the object in his hand. "It's been stuck at the Iron Grave for 15 years... Malos left it there."
"He didn't just leave it... he feared it!" the old man stammers. "That tuning fork is the one piece built to be a 'Safety Switch' — in case the Antenna system malfunctions. It's the only thing in the universe that can command all the waves of silence to 'decohere' in an instant. No wonder... no wonder he was willing to do anything to lure you here — because he needs to destroy this key before he can activate the perfect Silence Generator!"
A while later, the Captain calls everyone together for a briefing: "Listen up, everyone. Malos lured us here because his Silence Generator doesn't have enough power to spread across the whole galaxy. It needs Nova as an Antenna to amplify its power, and it needs my Voice Print to flip the switch..."
The Captain points to the secret map the old curator has drawn up. "But it underestimated us. We'll go in through the underground air duct the old man told us about, and use the Heartbeat Drive to turn its own plan against it from the inside!"
Vale cuts in. "So who exactly is this old guy? Nobody's told me a thing."
"I'm the forgotten backup tuner..." the old man says between hacking coughs. "I watched Malos go mad and destroy his own daughter, and I'm the one who tried to sneak out a warning signal that day, which threw off the ritual... so he had me locked away here to die along with the cathedral's secrets. But it seems fate wants to give me a chance to make things right."

### 6. Into the Jaws of Death *(The Final Approach)*

The Starwave surges into the rainbow-colored storm surrounding Melodia. The Acolyte squadron closes in, herding and firing on them like wolves driving sheep into a pen. "Five minutes left on the countdown!" Koro shouts. "Nova is starting to react to the cathedral!"
"Let it herd us in!" the Captain roars, pulling the controls. "Vale, Lyra, get ready to fight in front of the cathedral the moment we hit the drop point! We're going to turn its home into a concert stage it will never forget!"
The Starwave dives toward the cathedral through a hail of gunfire, ready for the decisive battle staked on the music of the universe.

## ACT III — THE PERFECT RESONANCE *(The Rhythm that Fits)*
### 1. Catastrophe Above the Heavens *(The Great Discord)*

The sky above Melodia twists into what looks like a rainbow-colored scar tearing itself open. The Starwave shudders violently as it collides with the ever-intensifying wave of "The Discordant Hum."
"Malos's Silence Generator doesn't have enough power to reach the whole galaxy!" the old curator shouts over the warning sirens. "But it's waiting for Nova... if it can connect that droid to the cathedral's core, Nova will become an Antenna that amplifies this silence to the very edge of the universe!"
The Captain grits his teeth, watching the countdown of the Mass Dissolution Virus tick down to just 00:05:00. "It's not just waiting for Nova, it's waiting for my 'voice' too... Koro! Get ready to detach the lower section of the ship and dive into the underground air duct, now!"

### 2. The Diversionary Assault *(The Vanguard Diversion)*

As the Starwave closes in on the cathedral, the Acolyte squadron swarms in to surround them, exactly as Malos planned, herding them toward the front gate. "Now! Vale, Lyra, cover the top!" the Captain roars.
Part of the ship breaks off and dives down into the valley below, while Vale and Lyra leap onto the stone plaza in front of the cathedral to draw the enemy's attention. Vale twirls his heavy cannon, its concussive blasts sending up billows of pink smoke. "Come and get it, you silent bastards! Today, Daddy's throwing a concert loud enough to blow out your ears!" He erupts into maniacal laughter as Lyra dances with her lightblade, cutting through frequency rounds and deflecting them back to precisely destroy enemy war-droids. Every beat of their fight opens a gap for the Captain to sneak into the heart of the cathedral.

### 3. The Tuning Fork and the Truth Beneath the Mask *(The Altar of Truth)*

The Captain and Koro carry Nova through the underground air duct following the old man's directions, until they emerge in the center of the cathedral hall where Malos stands waiting on a black stone altar. "You're too late, Captain..." Malos says coldly, without turning to look. "The virus in that droid is about to activate, and when it explodes, its wave will merge with this cathedral and silence every breath in the galaxy!"
The Captain says nothing. He walks in and lays Nova on the cleansing altar, then raises the crystal tuning fork (Hardware Key). "You threw this away because you were disgusted by it, Malos... but you forgot that the key you abandoned in the junk heap is the only thing that can access this cathedral's Admin system!"
The Captain slowly removes his chrome mask, revealing the face of the "scavenger kid" Malos once looked down on fifteen years ago. "You think I walked right into your trap? No... I came to change your rhythm instead." He plugs the tuning fork into the hidden port at the back of Nova's neck. Instantly, the holographic monitors around the cathedral flash bright red, displaying the message Koro had once encountered: [WAITING FOR THE MISSING CHORD]

### 4. "My Name Is... Elias" *(The Master Key)*

Malos freezes at the sight of that message. "The password I created... no one in this entire universe can complete a melody that has already been destroyed!"
"You're wrong, Malos..." the Captain closes his eyes, drawing a deep breath. He isn't just recalling his own name — he's recalling the "rhythm of life" he built together with Nova amid the junk heap. Elias erupts into a heavy, precise beatbox that weaves together with the cathedral's ringing hum in an astonishing way.
His heartbeat pounds so hard the sensor system registers it, and in the moment the melody hits its peak, Elias lets out a roar that answers every mystery:
"The note you lost... it's the sound of the person you left behind to die, isn't it! My name is Elias, and I am this droid's brother!"
The instant he finishes speaking, the red letters turn pure gold: [MISSING CHORD FOUND: AUTHENTICATION COMPLETE]. Golden light bursts from Nova's body as the Mass Dissolution Virus is purged by the correct code. Nova's eyes open, the light within shifting from glaring red to pure gold. The little droid rises into the air, sending out an enormous Resonance Pulse that surges back toward Malos's Silence Generator, cracking it apart.
> **[Pacing Note: The Final Struggle]** Malos roars in a fit of madness. "No! I won't let that filthy sound come back and destroy the silence I built!" He rips out his frequency blade and lunges at the little droid. But... the beatbox melody forms into a shimmering curtain of vibration, and the sound wave seeps through his mask into ears that have been deaf for so many years.
Malos freezes, the blade falling from his hand and clattering onto the stone floor. Its ringing sound is the first thing he has truly "heard" in fifteen years. He sinks to the ground, clutching his head, and the crack in his mask reveals eyes brimming with tears as the golden melody slowly condenses into an image of Myra, walking up to embrace him from behind. The rigid hatred melts away in an instant, dissolved by the heartbeat he had long forgotten.

### 5. Epilogue: The Melody That Never Ends
The terrifying silence fades away, replaced by birdsong and music drifting in from every direction across the universe.
> **[Pacing Note: The Moment of Stillness]** Elias walks toward Malos amid the ruins of the now-still cathedral. Everything falls silent... but it isn't the same empty silence as before. It's a silence that lets Malos hear the sound of his own "heartbeat" for the first time in 15 years.
Elias extends a hand to the man who once destroyed his life. "This galaxy is wide enough for every kind of song... even one that was once as broken as yours." Malos looks at the outstretched hand, then at Myra's shadow slowly fading away in the sunlight. He doesn't answer with words, but a single tear falls and strikes the stone floor, ringing out like the final note of redemption.
Aboard the Starwave, soaring back up into space, Elias sits at the controls with no mask concealing his identity anymore. Nova flies loops around him, beatboxing playful challenges at Vale and Lyra. The old curator sits humming an ancient tune, content. Koro asks the old man where he'd like to be dropped off, but the old man simply says, "I think I'll stay aboard this ship for a while. I've got nowhere else to go anyway."
[Final Pacing: The Fading Echo] The camera slowly pulls back from the Starwave as it heads toward a new horizon. The score gradually fades until all that remains is Elias's soft beatbox, weaving together with Nova's electronic hum...
At that moment, Vale chimes in. "Hey, Captain, we've been together this whole time and you never once mentioned this whole 'Elias' thing." Hearing this, Elias smiles and replies, "Isn't it easier to just call me Captain?" Everyone bursts out laughing at his answer.
It's a song that began in a junkyard... and now it rings out to the very edge of the universe, a symbol of a freedom that was never truly silenced.

### Anticipated Reception

**RESONANCE I: The Missing Chord** (November 2028) is in development as of April 2026. The following reflects pre-production buzz and critical framework.

**Creative team profile:**
The combination of James Hwang (creator, co-director, composer, lead actor), Kogonada (co-director), and Ludwig Göransson (score) represents an unusually credentialed above-the-line team for an original IP science fiction film. Kogonada's features — *Columbus* (2017) and *After Yang* (2022) — averaged 95% and 97% Rotten Tomatoes respectively. Göransson's score work includes *Oppenheimer* and the Black Panther franchise. ILM is attached for visual effects.

**Industry framing:**
The film is being positioned as a prestige sci-fi entry rather than a franchise-first tentpole — a positioning that has drawn comparisons to *Arrival* and *Everything Everywhere All at Once* in trade coverage. James Hwang's DECADE-era MV "NEW" (directed by Kogonada) has been cited as proof-of-concept for the visual language the film will pursue.

**Awards potential:**
Pre-production Oscar buzz exists in the following categories: Original Score (Göransson), Original Screenplay (Hwang), and Best Picture — contingent on critical reception at time of release. The Sundance and Venice festival circuits are considered likely exhibition targets given the film's auteur positioning.

**Context note:** This is an anticipated entry. Critical scores and box office figures will be updated upon release.
