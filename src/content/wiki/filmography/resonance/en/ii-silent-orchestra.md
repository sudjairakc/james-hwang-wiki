## ACT I — The Echo of Extinction

The Dying World: The film opens with a bone-chilling silence on a planet that has been wiped clean by the High Cantor. The Starwave team arrives and finds the "Living Statues" — people frozen mid-gesture, in an atmosphere with no wind and no sound of any living creature.

The Ambush: A fleet of silent ships emerges from an empty dimension. The Starwave tries to defend itself with Resonance waves, but the High Cantor unleashes "The Void Pulse," which devours all sonic energy. The Starwave begins to break apart.

The Destruction: A ferocious escape action sequence. Koro tries to salvage the engine systems but is struck by an electrical short and pinned under falling metal. Nova, acting as a living Black Box, absorbs the melodic Log of everyone's life-signature and signals for everyone to jump into the escape pods before the Starwave explodes in the middle of space.

## ACT II — The Weaver in the Glass

The Burial Ground: The team crash-lands on Sound Vault, a planet whose surface is covered in light-reflecting glass crystals. Elias wakes to find the team scattered. He finds Mina, who is using a "thread of light" to weave together the echoes of the planet's dead.

The Reunion: Mina guides Elias to the others at an ancient temple. Orpheus (who has also survived) begins acting suspiciously — he walks along the temple walls, whispering to the stones as if speaking to an old acquaintance.

The Mission: Orpheus reveals the blueprint of the "RESONANCE ship," an ancient warship — a relic from the war between the Resonance civilization and the power of Silence, buried underground. Its propulsion system requires "sonic filament ore" and a "pure frequency core," which must be sought out at various points across the planet.

## ACT III — The Scavenger's Symphony

The Exploration: The team splits up to scavenge materials to build the ship.

Elias & Mina — The Echo Forest: The pair must collect "sonic filament ore" in a glass forest that endlessly reflects and amplifies noise. Mina teaches Elias to use rhythmic breathing to drown out the interference. Elias begins to see that Mina is the loneliest of them all, because she hears the voices of the dead all the time.

Koro & Orpheus — The Mechanical Tomb: The injured Koro struggles to assemble the engine. Orpheus teaches him "Techno-Sympathy" — the technique of listening to the stress in metal. Koro begins to notice a strange tattoo on Orpheus's arm, resembling the symbols found on the enemy's machines.

Vale & Lyra — The Resonance Spires: The pair must climb a mountain peak to seize the "frequency core," fighting a fierce battle against a spider-shaped Guardian machine that uses shockwaves. Lyra begins wielding the Force more aggressively to protect Vale, whose gun is running low on ammunition.

The Assembly: A montage sequence of building the RESONANCE ship. The vessel gradually grows like a living, mechanical creature. The team begins to laugh again and feel hope once more.

## ACT IV — The Muting & The Betrayal

The Final Piece: The new ship still lacks an "energy core." The team decides to raid a Void station floating above the planet.

The Horror: At the heart of the station, the High Cantor appears. He walks past Lyra and Vale, who try in vain to stop him. He stops in front of Elias and reaches out to touch his forehead. Suddenly, the film's musical score vanishes completely. Elias is plunged into a world of absolute silence, gripped by panic and unable to act.

The Great Twist: The High Cantor calls Orpheus "father." Orpheus lowers his head and admits that he was the one who conducted this flawed experiment, and that he had been secretly feeding coordinates to the enemy all along in order to finally "close the job." Orpheus walks toward the High Cantor and coldly abandons the team.

The Chaos: The station begins to collapse. Koro tries to salvage the energy core, triggering an explosion that costs him his arm for real. Vale and Lyra are pulled into a containment dimension by the power of the Void, and Mina is seized by the High Cantor to be used as an "amplifier for the signal of silence."

## ACT V — The Silent Resilience

The Darkness of Soul: Elias sits motionless amid the wreckage, hearing nothing at all. His world becomes a terrifying, mute picture. He is on the verge of giving up.

The Connection: Nova crawls toward him, badly damaged, and places a hand on Elias's chest, sending a vibration signal in the beatbox rhythm of the very first captain. Elias feels the heartbeat rhythm of Nova and the rest of his friends.

The Awakening: Elias gathers himself. He cannot hear with his ears, but he can "feel" with his mind. He helps Koro and Nova aboard the RESONANCE and issues commands through the ship's vibration system.

Ending Frame: The RESONANCE ship blasts off into the atmosphere. Elias's face is still, calm, and fiercely determined. He looks out and watches the stars go dark one by one, leaving the universe in darkness. The film ends with the sound of a single heartbeat from Elias before the screen cuts to black.
