# Realism Assessment — Does this read like a real artist wiki?

Verdict from the 6-domain sweep: **high-tier for a fictional/fanfic wiki**, above most,
with a handful of specific "tells" that break the illusion. Below: what already works,
what strains, and the concrete fixes that would push it toward Wikipedia-grade credibility.

## Overall grade

| Dimension | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Internal consistency | B | Strong spine (3.0B stream total sums clean; pictorial/fashion dates match 1:1). Undercut by ~10 factual contradictions (see conflicts.md). |
| Numeric realism | B− | Endorsement bands, box office, tour economics are believable; broken by per-track > album stream math and touring-capital arithmetic. |
| Self-awareness | A | Rare strength — the wiki critiques itself (pace problem, MJ-reliance debate, "for a K-pop artist" backhanded praise, reference-vocalist seam). This is what real, mature artist coverage does. |
| Structural completeness | C+ | Reads like feature articles, not a reference DB — missing the "boring" infobox data real wikis carry (certifications, chart runs, runtimes, registration numbers). |
| Real-world plausibility | C | A K-pop idol as MCU lead + co-writer + Disney franchise creator/co-director/composer + CEO is the central swing; the MJ-Estate/Captain-EO rights chain is the least plausible single element. |

## What already reads authentic (keep)

- **Graduated authorship arc** (topliner → co-producer → sole producer → lead-with-collaborators) mirrors real producer-artists (The-Dream, Frank Ocean). Believable.
- **Self-critical distance:** the "Slow Down" pace critique, the MJ-reliance for/against debate, the reference-vocalist seam being *named in-canon*. Real coverage has dissent; hagiography doesn't.
- **Thai-Chinese-Korean identity** is specific and grounded (Bangkok open-call, bilingual-advantage judge note, SM Rookies Thailand showcase).
- **Exhaustion beats** (Feb 2024 "utterly exhausted", therapist since 2023) humanize an otherwise superhuman schedule.
- **Endorsement calibration** ($1.5–7M/deal, luxury tier) is the most realistically-priced section.
- **Reception essays** pre-empt the "diversity-quota Grammy" criticism and cite counter-examples (Pharrell correcting authorship) instead of asserting unanimous acclaim.

## What strains credibility (address)

1. **Real people + fabricated specifics (biggest risk).** Karina, Hanni, Jungkook, Mark, Ashley Liao are real; the wiki asserts specific *romantic relationships* ("confirmed by sources close to the parties"). Reads defamation-shaped even in admitted fiction. Same issue: L.I.N.A. lists real aespa/NewJeans/Le Sserafim/IVE members by name in a fictional project. **Recommend:** either (a) fictionalize these names, or (b) add a prominent in-universe disclaimer and keep relationship claims vague/"rumored, unconfirmed" only.
2. **The Captain EO / MJ-Estate rights chain.** Disney + Lucasfilm + MJ Estate + a boutique idol company co-owning a *new* theatrical extension of MJ's 1986 Captain EO, with a posthumous MJ cameo, is far from how the real (protective) MJ Estate operates. It's internally consistent but plausibility-thin. Keep as deliberate genre-fiction, but don't lean on it as "realistic".
3. **Overloaded creative-control claims.** Simultaneously writing/directing/scoring/starring a Disney trilogy + acting a Marvel trilogy + a 6-album solo catalog + full NCT 127 reintegration. The overlapping production windows (RESONANCE II post + III photography both 2030) are the tell. **Recommend:** thin one lane (e.g. co-director not sole-director; or drop the composer credit to "music consultant").
4. **"Too clean" image.** `public-image.ts` asserts "no negative reports about his personal character from any source" across a 10-year, high-press career. Real wikis have at least one walked-back/contested incident. **Recommend:** add one minor, resolved near-miss (an old tweet, a mistranslation controversy) to make the clean record earned.
5. **Numeric tells** (fix via conflicts A4, A11, A12): singles out-streaming their album; touring capital that exceeds its profit pool. Real press-kit numbers don't do this.
6. **Boutique company → $420–650M valuation** on one artist is aggressive without outside capital/diversification. The valuation hinges on the (fictional) Captain-EO rights, so it stands or falls with #2.
7. **Compressed legal win.** Full IP + trademark from SM in ~5 months, "both withdrew, no damages" is tidier than real K-pop disputes (which ran 1–2+ years). Fine as simplification; add friction if you want grit.
8. **Age-math + military-service handwave.** Timeline ages ignore the June birthday (half the entries off by one); Thai conscription "process 2018→exemption 2019" is longer than real Thai lottery mechanics, and the "why not Korean enlistment" link (he's Thai) is never stated.

## The single highest-leverage realism move

**Add the reference-database layer real wikis live on** — the "boring" structured data (certifications, chart-run tables, runtimes, ratings, credits granularity, registration/PRO info). Feature-essay prose is already strong; what's missing is the infobox/tabular substrate that makes readers trust a wiki. See [creative-opportunities.md](creative-opportunities.md) §"Missing data".

## Where it already beats a typical fanfic wiki

- Bilingual parity, cross-page date consistency (pictorials↔fashion), a clean top-line stream total, and — most of all — **built-in criticism of its own subject**. Most fan wikis are pure praise; this one argues with itself, which is the most "real" thing about it.
