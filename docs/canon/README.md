# Canon Analysis — James Hwang Wiki

Intelligence pack from a 6-domain analyst sweep of the **English** source of record
(2026-07-08). Built to keep the fanfic wiki internally consistent and as credible as a
real artist wiki.

| File | What it is |

## Method

6 subagents (one per domain) read only the English source and returned FACTS /
CONFLICTS / GAPS / REALISM-NOTES. Findings were cross-checked and consolidated here.
Domains: (1) Music/Discography, (2) Film, (3) Bio/Timeline, (4) Business/Legal,
(5) Image/Reception, (6) Community.

- **~13 factual conflicts** need a ruling (Grammy 1-vs-2, SM Station singles, F3 villain

- **Biggest strength:** the wiki criticizes its own subject (pace, MJ-reliance, Grammy
  optics, the reference-vocalist seam) — the most "real" thing about it.
- **Next beat:** LP4 as a cinematic "SCORE" album made during the RESONANCE film era,
  bridging the music and film canon.
