## 1. Next album — concept options

The discography arc has a clear shape and a clear **gap**:

```
LIKE I DO ── SOFTEST TOUCH ── PULSE ── ARE U STILL ── CAN'T LEAVE ALONE ── DECADE
seed          relaunch         apex-1   apex-2 (peak)   recalibration       anthology
2016          2022             2023     2024            2025                2026 (closes ch.1)
```

DECADE deliberately closes the 10-year chapter. The next studio album (**LP4**, ~2027–2028) should open chapter 2, and it lands **during the RESONANCE film era** — so it can be the first record made by "James the auteur/film-composer", not "James the idol-in-exile". Three concept directions:

### Option A — "SCORE" / cinematic soul (recommended)
- **Hook:** first album written in parallel with scoring RESONANCE; orchestral-electronic R&B, Göransson-adjacent texture, his falsetto over film-scale arrangements. Bridges music ↔ film canon (which currently barely touch).
- **Realism fit:** matches the real trajectory of artists who move into scoring (e.g. the Weeknd/After Hours cinematic turn). Explains the 2-yr gap after DECADE (he was making a film).
- **Era color:** deep blue/void palette → ties to RESONANCE #3a5a7c.

### Option B — "HOME" / roots record
- **Hook:** first Thai-language-forward album; Bangkok recording, mor-lam/luk-thung textures under R&B; addresses the 2025 Thai-political fallout head-on. Reclaims the identity thread the wiki keeps raising but never resolves musically.
- **Realism fit:** strong — diaspora artists doing a "roots" record is a well-worn, credible beat; also deepens the underused Thai fanbase (17% of fandom).

### Option C — "LIVE BAND" / analog left turn
- **Hook:** fully live-band, one-take, no-grid record (extends the ARE U STILL "humanized rhythm" thread); recorded to tape; a deliberate anti-pop statement post-fame.
- **Realism fit:** the D'Angelo/Voodoo move; fits the self-critical "restraint = power" thesis.

> Recommendation: **A (SCORE)** as LP4 (bridges music+film, explains the gap), holding **B (HOME)** as LP5 — the eventual "return to self" after the franchise years.

## 3. Missing DATA (highest realism-per-effort)

These are the "boring" facts that make a wiki *feel* real. Ranked by leverage:

1. **Certifications** for every release (Gold/Platinum, RIAA + Circle/Gaon). Biggest single credibility lift.
2. **Chart-run tables** (weekly peaks over N weeks) instead of one peak figure per album.
3. **Runtimes, MPAA/BBFC ratings, budgets** for all 6 films; **composer** for the Shang-Chi trilogy (currently none vs RESONANCE's Göransson).
4. **Mix/master engineers** and **publishing administrator / PRO** (KOMCA + ASCAP/BMI) — you emphasize authorship but never name who mixes or administers.
5. **"As-of" dates** on all follower counts and a single denominator for the fandom-size % table.
6. **Award ceremony dates + host cities** for the 2022–2026 music awards (only the acting awards have dates).
7. **Company registration** (entity type "Co., Ltd.", reg. no., HQ district), **investor names** behind the 10% stake, **ticketing partner**, **merch manufacturer**.
8. **Sample clearance** for MYSTERY LADY (interpolates CeCe Peniston "Movin' On") — writer split + clearance credit.
9. **Vinyl/physical variants** + packaging/photobook specs (esp. for DECADE the "collector object").
10. **SM Station 1** (only 2 & 3 exist — numbered gap) and the **unnamed "second Thai trainee"** thread.
